Overall Target
Minimum: 150 questions
Good: 200 questions
Very Strong for MERN Remote Jobs: 250–300 questions

Lekin ek condition hai:

❌ 300 random questions solve mat karna.

✅ Har pattern ko samajh kar solve karna.

Example:

Two Sum → HashMap
Contains Duplicate → HashSet
Valid Anagram → HashMap
Group Anagrams → HashMap

Ye 4 questions alag lagte hain, lekin pattern same hai. Jab pattern samajh aa gaya to 10 aur questions automatically easy lagne lagte hain.

Mera suggestion:

Roz 2–3 LeetCode questions.
Pehle Arrays + HashMap ke 50–60 questions.
Phir Two Pointers + Sliding Window ke 30–40.
Phir Binary Search + Linked List.

Agar tum 6 mahine mein lagbhag 200 achhe questions kar lete ho aur saath mein apne MERN projects bhi strong rakhte ho, to remote MERN interviews ke liye kaafi competitive position mein aa sakte ho.
